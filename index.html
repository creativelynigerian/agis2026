Empowering Young People for Global Impact

Accelerate Global Impact Summit (AGIS) is an annual youth empowerment event designed to instill Christ, Character, Competence and Capacity in young people. Since its inception in 2011, AGIS has inspired and challenged loads of young people to discover purpose, develop their potential and make meaningful impact in their generation.

AGIS 2026 Theme: FRESH OIL — For Innovative Thinking. A fresh outpouring for a generation ready to think differently, create boldly, and lead with clarity.

Our Foundation

Four Pillars of AGIS

Building young people from the inside out

AGIS is built around four foundational values that shape young people for purposeful living, leadership and positive influence. The summit combines spiritual formation with practical learning, technology, purpose discovery and capacity development.

Christ

Faith, spiritual growth and a life centred on Jesus Christ.

Character

Integrity, discipline, values and responsible leadership.

Competence

Skills, technology, confidence and the ability to create lasting impact.

Capacity

Growing the inner strength to sustain meaningful, long-term impact.
